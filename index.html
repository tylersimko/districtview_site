DistrictView

Data science tools to study US school board policy-making

DistrictView is the largest collection of school board meetings ever assembled and publicly released. We identify, process, clean, and publicly release data on school board meetings as posted on YouTube.

DistrictView was created by Tyler Simko, Rebecca Johnson, Mirya Holman, and Mason Reece. DistrictView is a companion project to LocalView, by Soubhik Barari, Tyler Simko, and Mason Reece.
